# Personalized Email Outreach Template

**Subject: Quick question about [Company Name]'s product data**

Hi [First Name],

I was on your website and noticed you have a great selection of products. I can only imagine how much work goes into managing all of that product data, especially when you're selling on multiple channels.

I'm the founder of [Your Company Name], and I've spent the last [Number] years helping companies like yours streamline their product data workflows. We've built a tool that helps you import product data from any file format, automatically map it to your system, and validate it for accuracy.

I know you're busy, but I'd love to show you how we can save you [Number] hours per week and eliminate data errors. Would you be open to a quick 15-minute chat next week?

Best,

[Your Name]

---

## Notes on Personalization

*   **[Company Name]:** The name of the prospect's company.
*   **[First Name]:** The first name of the prospect.
*   **[Number] years:** The number of years you've been working in the industry.
*   **[Number] hours:** The number of hours you estimate you can save them per week. This should be based on your research of their company.

## Why This Template Works

*   **It's personalized:** It shows that you've done your research and you're not just sending a generic email.
*   **It's focused on value:** It highlights the benefits of your product, not just the features.
*   **It's short and to the point:** It respects the prospect's time.
*   **It has a clear call to action:** It makes it easy for the prospect to say yes.
